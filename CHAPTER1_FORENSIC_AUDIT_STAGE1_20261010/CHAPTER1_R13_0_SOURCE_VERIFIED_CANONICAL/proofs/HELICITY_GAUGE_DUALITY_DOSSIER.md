# Helicity, gauge, duality — dossier (R13.0)

## Gauge

\(\mathbf A'=\mathbf A+\nabla\chi\), \(\phi'=\phi-j\omega\chi\) under \(e^{+j\omega t}\).

\[
\mathbf E'=-\nabla\phi'-j\omega\mathbf A'=\mathbf E,\qquad
\mathbf B'=\nabla\times\mathbf A'=\mathbf B.
\]

**Status:** VERIFIED BY DERIVATION.

## Helicity density (manuscript)

\[
h(\mathbf r)=\frac{1}{2\omega c}\Im\{\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf H^*\}.
\]

CP plane wave \(\mathbf E=(E_0/\sqrt2)(\hat{\mathbf x}+j\sigma\hat{\mathbf y})e^{-jkz}\), \(\mathbf H=\hat{\mathbf z}\times\mathbf E/\eta_0\):

\[
h=\frac{\sigma\varepsilon_0 E_0^2}{2\omega}
\]

for the stated amplitude convention (manuscript Eq. `ch1.helicity-plane-wave`).

**Scope:** Field scalar record; not Lipkin zilch, not Stokes \(S_3\), not canonical-potential helicity.

## Duality

Source-free homogeneous region, uniform \(\varepsilon,\mu\):

\[
\mathbf E'=\cos\xi\,\mathbf E+\sin\xi\,\eta\mathbf H,\quad
\mathbf H'=-\sin\xi\,\mathbf E/\eta+\cos\xi\,\mathbf H.
\]

Curl equations preserved with \(\eta=\sqrt{\mu/\varepsilon}\).

**Scope (manuscript explicit):** Does not extend to impressed sources, jumping \(\eta\), or arbitrary interfaces without dual-covariant boundary data.
