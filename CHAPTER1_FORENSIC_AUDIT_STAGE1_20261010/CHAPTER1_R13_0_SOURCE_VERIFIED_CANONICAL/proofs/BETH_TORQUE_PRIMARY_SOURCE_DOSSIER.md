## Original equations (Gaussian units, Beth)

- **(1)** Torque density: $\boldsymbol\tau=\mathbf P\times\mathbf E=(\mathbf D\times\mathbf E)/(4\pi)$ when $\mu=1$.
- **(2)** Plane wave in anisotropic crystal along $+z$ with principal dielectric tensor components.
- **(3)** Time-averaged torque per unit area between $z_1$ and $z_2$ (half-wave-plate limit quoted in abstract).
- **(6)** Vacuum angular-momentum flux per unit area per second for elliptical components (quantum section).

## Independent SI map (qualified)

Beth's Gaussian $\boldsymbol\tau=\mathbf P\times\mathbf E$ maps to SI material torque density with $\mathbf P=\varepsilon_0\chi\cdot\mathbf E$ (and macroscopic constitutive care for anisotropic $\varepsilon$). No claim in Chapter 1 that vacuum $\mathbf T_{\mathrm{Maxwell}}$ evaluated in the crystal reproduces $\mathbf P\times\mathbf E$ pointwise.
