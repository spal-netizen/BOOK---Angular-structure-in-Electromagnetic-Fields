## Counter-rotating cancellation (§1.6.2)

For amplitudes \(A_\pm\) with \(P_\pm=|A_\pm|^2/(4\eta_0)\) and equal powers \(P_+=P_-\):

\[
\dot J_{z,\mathrm{orb,out}}=\frac{m}{\omega}(P_+-P_-)=0
\]

for the **orbital bookkeeping** scale cited in text.
