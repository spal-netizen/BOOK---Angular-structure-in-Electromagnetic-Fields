**SI peak phasors, \(\exp(+j\omega t)\):** Eq. `beth-torque-cycle-average`
\[
\boldsymbol\tau(t)=\mathbf P(t)\times\mathbf E(t),\qquad
\langle\boldsymbol\tau\rangle=\tfrac12\operatorname{Re}\{\mathbf P\times\mathbf E^*\}.
\]
