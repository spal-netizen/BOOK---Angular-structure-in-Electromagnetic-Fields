Batch B review build — Volume I integration (Ch.1–4, refs resolved)
================================================================

Prerequisites: TeX Live with latexmk, bibtex/biber as configured in latex/.

From repository root:

  cd latex
  latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -file-line-error main-vol1-stage10.tex

On success, outputs in latex/:
  main-vol1-stage10.pdf

Copy to this directory as VOLUME_I_REVIEW.pdf (see build-review-batch-b.ps1).

Chapter-only review copies: extract pages with your PDF tool, or compile with
main-vol1-stage10.pdf as the authoritative cross-reference-complete document.

Chapter 1 benchmark is included from protected ch01.tex; verify SHA per
VOLUME_I_RELEASE/CHAPTER1_BENCHMARK_MANIFEST.txt after any full build.
