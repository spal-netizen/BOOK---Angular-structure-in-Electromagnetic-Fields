\section{Radiation Operators and Observable Fields}
\label{sec:ch02.6}

\subsection{Outgoing radiative extraction}
\label{sec:ch02.6.1}

A completed Maxwell pair $(\E,\H)=\mathcal{S}_\omega[\Jimp]$ generally contains reactive storage, evanescent structure tied to the near zone, and---when the exterior is open---propagating waves that may be incoming or outgoing. Far-zone measurements respond to a narrower class: outgoing transverse fields whose leading spatial dependence is $e^{-\jj k_0 r}/r$. The map $\Pi_{\mathrm{prop}}$ retains propagating spectral content on a homogeneous background; the map $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{out}}$ selects the outgoing branch of that content (Section~\ref{sec:ch02.4.2}). Their composition
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ch2.Pirad-composition-repeat}
\mathcal{R}_\omega=\Pi_{\mathrm{rad}}\circ\mathcal{S}_\omega^{\mathrm{(out)}},\ 
\mathcal{S}_\omega^{\mathrm{(out)}}=\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{out}}\circ\mathcal{S}_\omega
\end{equation}
is the \emph{outgoing radiative extraction map} acting on field pairs in $\mathcal{F}_\Omega$. It is not an orthogonal Hilbert projection on all of $\mathcal{F}_\Omega$ unless an inner product and closed subspace are specified; physically it implements the radiation condition on propagating components. On the radiative subspace $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{rad}}=\operatorname{ran}\Pi_{\mathrm{rad}}$, one has $\Pi_{\mathrm{rad}}[\mathbf{u}]=\mathbf{u}$ and $\Pi_{\mathrm{rad}}^2=\Pi_{\mathrm{rad}}$ when both sides are understood on $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{rad}}$.

The \emph{radiation operator} composes source excitation with that extraction:
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ch2.Romega-composition-repeat}
\mathcal{R}_\omega=\Pi_{\mathrm{rad}}\circ\mathcal{S}_\omega^{\mathrm{(out)}}:\mathcal{J}_\Omega\longrightarrow\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{rad}},
\qquad
\mathcal{S}_\omega^{\mathrm{(out)}}=\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{out}}\circ\mathcal{S}_\omega,\quad
\mathcal{R}_\omega[\Jimp]=\Pi_{\mathrm{rad}}\big(\mathcal{S}_\omega^{\mathrm{(out)}}[\Jimp]\big).
\end{equation}
Physically, $\mathcal{S}_\omega$ is the forward Maxwell calculation for a prescribed admissible source; $\mathcal{R}_\omega$ is the same calculation followed by retention of outgoing radiative export only.

Linearity on fixed boundary data implies, for $a_1,a_2\in\mathbb{C}$ and $\Jimp^{(1)},\Jimp^{(2)}\in\mathcal{J}_\Omega$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ch2.R-linearity}
\mathcal{R}_\omega[a_1\Jimp^{(1)}+a_2\Jimp^{(2)}]
=a_1\mathcal{R}_\omega[\Jimp^{(1)}]+a_2\mathcal{R}_\omega[\Jimp^{(2)}].
\end{equation}
The range $\operatorname{ran}\mathcal{R}_\omega$ is strictly smaller than $\operatorname{ran}\mathcal{S}_\omega$ whenever reactive storage is nontrivial: distinct near-zone states can share identical far-zone patterns (Section~\ref{sec:ch02.8}).

\subsection[Measurement and the map \texorpdfstring{$\Qomega$}{Q\_omega}]{Measurement and the map $\Qomega$}
\label{sec:ch02.6.2}

A far-zone instrument does not return the full field $\mathcal{R}_\omega[\Jimp]$. It returns a finite list of numbers---pattern samples, polarization ratios, or calibrated voltages---obtained through probes with finite aperture and finite angular sampling (Section~\ref{sec:ch01.5}). Let $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{far}}$ denote a linear functional extracting a declared far-zone scalar or vector quantity from $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{rad}}$, and let $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{meas}}:\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{rad}}\to\mathbb{C}^{N}$ model the probe bank. The laboratory map
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:ch2.Qomega-repeat}
\Qomega=\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{meas}}\circ\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{far}}\circ\mathcal{R}_\omega
\end{equation}
assigns to each admissible $\Jimp$ a measurement vector in $\mathbb{C}^{N}$. Rank limitations of $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{meas}}$ cap how many independent radiative degrees of freedom can be resolved regardless of Maxwell correctness \cite{harrington2001,hansen1988}.
