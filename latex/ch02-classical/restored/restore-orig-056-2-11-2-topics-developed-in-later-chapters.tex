\subsection{Topics developed in later chapters}
\phantomsection\label{sec:ch02112}

Green-function inversion on bounded sources, outgoing selection, and radiation operators supply the
harmonic Maxwell framework on which later chapters impose additional coordinates and realizability
constraints \cite{stratton1941,harrington2001}. Chapter~\ref{ch:03} introduces vector spherical
harmonics and multipole fields as an explicit angular basis for $\mathcal{R}_\omega$, including
orthogonality on $S^2$ and watt-calibrated modal amplitudes \cite{stratton1941,hansen1988}.
Chapter~\ref{ch:04} couples those modes to impressed currents, finite electrical size, and
source-equivalence bounds that lie beyond the operator layer developed here \cite{harrington2001}.

Subjects requiring full harmonic tables, realizability proofs on $\mathcal{J}_\Omega$, or formal
interaction theory are cited in Chapters~\ref{ch:03}--\ref{ch:04} without local re-derivation.
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:ch2.defer-topic-rule}--\eqref{eq:ch2.defer-reporting-lock} record that deferral
convention: vector-harmonic completion, $\mathbf{J}$-operator theory, and Volume~II interaction
formalism are forward pointers, not gaps in the present proofs \cite{stratton1941}.
