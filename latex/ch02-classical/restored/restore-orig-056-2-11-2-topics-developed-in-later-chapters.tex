\subsection{Topics developed in later chapters}
\phantomsection\label{sec:ch02112}
Green-function inversion on bounded sources, outgoing selection, and radiation operators reduce Maxwell's local laws to measurable fields on $\Gamma_R$.
Chapter~\ref{ch:03} replaces the operator-coordination language $\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{rad}}$ with explicit vector spherical harmonics: it derives orthogonality, multipole fields and watt-calibrated modal amplitudes that Chapter~\ref{ch:04} couples to impressed currents and finite-size limits \cite{stratton1941,hansen1988}.
Topics that require full harmonic tables, realizability proofs on $\mathcal{J}_\Omega$, or interaction theory beyond Volume~I are cited in those chapters rather than re-derived here \cite{harrington2001}.
