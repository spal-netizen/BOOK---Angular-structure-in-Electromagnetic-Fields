\section{Radiation Conditions and Uniqueness}
\label{sec:ch24}

Green-function convolution in Section~\ref{sec:ch23} constructs a harmonic field pair from impressed currents, but on unbounded exterior domains the Helmholtz--Maxwell problem still admits incoming as well as outgoing partners at infinity \cite{jackson1999,sommerfeld1949}. The present section fixes the outgoing branch that physical radiation problems require: impressed currents on finite support launch retarded waves whose far-zone power and pattern are determined only after that branch is selected \cite{stratton1941}.

Subsection~\ref{sec:ch241} states the Sommerfeld field condition; Subsection~\ref{sec:ch242} defines the outgoing selector $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{out}}$ and its distinction from far-amplitude extraction $\Pi_{\mathrm{rad}}$; Subsection~\ref{sec:ch243} proves that, on the outgoing branch, impressed data determine radiative export under the hypotheses imported from Chapter~\ref{ch:01}; Subsection~\ref{sec:ch244} records source non-uniqueness and reconstruction limits when reactive storage is misidentified as radiation \cite{devaney2004,harrington2001}.
