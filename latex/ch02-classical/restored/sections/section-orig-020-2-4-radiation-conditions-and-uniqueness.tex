\section{Radiation Conditions and Uniqueness}
\label{sec:ch24}
Green-function convolution in Section~\ref{sec:ch23} constructs a harmonic field pair from impressed
currents, but on unbounded exterior domains the Helmholtz--Maxwell problem still admits incoming as well
as outgoing partners at infinity \cite{jackson1999,sommerfeld1949}.
Section~\ref{sec:ch24} fixes the outgoing branch that laboratory practice already assumes: finite feeds,
anechoic chambers, and wattmeters on $\Gamma_R$ attribute power to sources that launch retarded waves
rather than to unspecified energy arriving from infinity \cite{stratton1941}.
Subsection~\ref{sec:ch241} states the Sommerfeld field condition; Subsection~\ref{sec:ch242} defines the
outgoing selector $\mathcal{O}_{\mathrm{out}}$ and its distinction from far-amplitude extraction
$\Pi_{\mathrm{rad}}$; Subsection~\ref{sec:ch243} proves that, on the outgoing branch, impressed data
determine radiative export up to the imported retarded-resolvent hypotheses; Subsection~\ref{sec:ch244} records
source non-uniqueness and reconstruction limits when reactive storage is misidentified as radiation
\cite{devaney2004,harrington2001}.
