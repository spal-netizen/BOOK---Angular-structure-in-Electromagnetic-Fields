\section{Time- and Frequency-Domain Maxwell Operators}
\label{sec:ch22}

Section~\ref{sec:ch21} fixed the function spaces and operator domains on which impressed sources and field pairs are defined. The present section shows how the same Maxwell physics appears in time-domain evolution and in the harmonic operators used when a single angular frequency $\omega$ is declared \cite{stratton1941}. Time-domain fields $\E(\mathbf{r},t)$ and $\H(\mathbf{r},t)$ make retarded causality explicit; phasor fields at fixed $\omega$ are steady-state reductions valid when excitation and media are sufficiently narrowband on the declared band \cite{harrington2001}. Each harmonic operator $\mathcal{L}_\omega$ is therefore a labelled cross-section of one causal law, not an independent postulate.

Subsection~\ref{sec:ch221} develops the time-domain Maxwell system and its retarded Green representation. Subsection~\ref{sec:ch222} packages the frequency-domain Maxwell operator and curl--curl Helmholtz reductions without re-displaying Eq.~\eqref{eq:ch1.maxwell-local}. Subsection~\ref{sec:ch223} states the equivalence between retarded propagation and the solution map $\mathcal{S}_\omega$ under the hypotheses fixed in Eq.~\eqref{eq:ch2.causality-retarded}. Subsection~\ref{sec:ch224} applies the reactive--radiative partition of Chapter~\ref{ch:01} to propagating, evanescent, and non-propagating subspaces in operator form \cite{stratton1941}.

Dyadic Green functions and inversion theory are deferred to Section~\ref{sec:ch23}, where the harmonic operators introduced here acquire explicit integral representations \cite{stratton1941}. Helmholtz and curl--curl reductions simplify kernel construction but do not replace the first-order $\mathcal{L}_\omega$ packaging of Eq.~\eqref{eq:ch2.maxwell-boundary-composite} when volume and boundary rows must be enforced jointly \cite{harrington2001}. Within a fixed harmonic band, $\mathcal{S}_\omega$ is the $\omega$-slice of a causal time-domain map whenever impressed data and $\mathcal{B}_\Gamma$ are held fixed across the transform \cite{stratton1941}. Modal and plane-wave decompositions in Subsection~\ref{sec:ch224} filter which components of $\mathcal{S}_\omega[\Jimp]$ may enter $\mathcal{R}_\omega$ under outgoing selection \cite{harrington2001}.
