\noindent\textbf{Harmonic convention.}\ 
Fields use $\Re\{\mathbf{F}(\mathbf{r})\,\eim\}$ with $\eim=\exp(-\jj\omega t)$, so $\partial_t\to-\jj\omega$ on phasors and Maxwell's equations are Eq.~\eqref{eq:ch1.maxwell-local} with charge closure Eq.~\eqref{eq:ch1.charge-continuity-harmonic}.
