# Proof closure checklist

Use once per HOME result before marking a subsection complete.

- [ ] **Domain:** geometry, medium class, frequency band, smoothness of fields
- [ ] **Sources:** impressed $\mathbf{J}$, $\rho$, or equivalent; distributional extensions declared
- [ ] **Boundaries:** PEC/PMC/impedance/passive loss; outward normal fixed
- [ ] **Radiation condition:** outgoing selection stated
- [ ] **Definitions:** all symbols in first appearance of each major equation block
- [ ] **Derivation:** no skipped sign steps in curls, Poynting, or stress divergences
- [ ] **Units:** SI dimensional consistency
- [ ] **Limiting cases:** e.g. lossless $\varepsilon,\mu$ real; $kr\ll1$; far zone
- [ ] **Independent check:** script, numeric spot check, or second derivation path
- [ ] **Theorem hypotheses ↔ conclusion:** no stronger conclusion than proved
- [ ] **Physical interpretation:** does not exceed mathematical result
- [ ] **Citation:** originator at claim; secondary only for standard identities
- [ ] **Printed equation:** matches TeX label and appears correctly in PDF build

## Red flags (stop and narrow)

- Using complex-medium dissipation identity on **real** lossless parameters without justification
- Identifying modal bookkeeping with a universal stress theorem
- Magnetic VSW expansion not paired with electric expansion under the active phasor sign
- Promoting ideal-mode $Q$ to arbitrary antennas
