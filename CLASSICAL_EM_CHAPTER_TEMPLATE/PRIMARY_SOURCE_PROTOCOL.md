# Primary source protocol

Apply at every **HOME** theorem, historical identity, or numeric benchmark.

## Six-step chain

1. **Identify** the original source (journal, report, edition, year).
2. **Locate** page, equation number, or section in that source.
3. **Record** the source's notation and time-harmonic convention.
4. **Convert** into the book's convention ($e^{+j\omega t}$, SI, outgoing $h^{(2)}$) with an independent derivation or limiting check.
5. **Verify** dimensions and a nontrivial limit (e.g. $ka\to0$, far zone, lossless limit).
6. **Cite** at the claim and state **scope** (ideal mode, geometry class, passive medium).

## Authentication levels (audit vocabulary—outside manuscript)

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Binary-verified | Authoritative PDF or scan; equation pinned to page |
| Text-verified | Readable HTML/metadata + cross-check against JAP or monograph |
| Secondary-checked | Harrington/Stratton/Jackson reproduction only |

Do not upgrade authentication level without new evidence.

## Chu / antenna-Q special case

- JAP 1948 + independent reproduction (McLean) suffices for **ideal $TM_{01}$** exact $Q$.
- MIT TR-064 details require binary pin before expanding circuit claims beyond JAP + McLean.

## When source is unavailable

- Retain only claims supported by a second primary source or a full in-chapter derivation.
- Otherwise qualify or remove.
